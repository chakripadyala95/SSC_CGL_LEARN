# Time & Work: cheat sheet

- [Combined work rate](/formulas/time-work#combined-rate): $\frac1T=\frac1a+\frac1b;\ T=\frac{ab}{a+b}$
  - Shortcut: Two workers: $T=\frac{ab}{a+b}$ (product over sum). If one is $k$ times faster than the other who takes $b$: $T=\frac{b}{k+1}$.
- [Total work as LCM; efficiency ratio](/formulas/time-work#lcm-total-work): $W=\mathrm{LCM}(t_i);\ e_i=\frac W{t_i};\ e_1:e_2=t_2:t_1$
  - Shortcut: Given an efficiency ratio $e_1:e_2$ and joint time $T$: $W=(e_1+e_2)T$, then $t_1=\frac W{e_1}$.
- [Inlets and outlets: net rate](/formulas/time-work#pipes-net-rate): $\frac1T=\Sigma\frac1{\text{fill}_i}-\Sigma\frac1{\text{empty}_j}$
  - Shortcut: Fill in $a$, with leak in $b$: leak alone empties in $\frac{ab}{b-a}$.
- [Joining, leaving and alternating](/formulas/time-work#partial-work): $\Sigma(\text{rate}_i\times\text{time worked}_i)=1;\ \text{alternating: work per cycle}=\Sigma\text{rates, then finish the remainder}$
  - Shortcut: Joined/left problems: write each person's working time in terms of the unknown and solve one linear equation.
- [Man-days-hours rule](/formulas/time-work#mdh-formula): $\frac{M_1D_1H_1}{W_1}=\frac{M_2D_2H_2}{W_2}$
