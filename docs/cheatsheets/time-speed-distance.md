# Time-Speed-Distance: cheat sheet

- [km/h to m/s conversion](/formulas/time-speed-distance#unit-conversion): $1\ \text{km/h}=\frac5{18}\ \text{m/s}$
  - Shortcut: km/h $\to$ m/s: $\times\frac5{18}$ (so 18 → 5, 36 → 10, 54 → 15, 72 → 20, 90 → 25); m/s $\to$ km/h: $\times\frac{18}5$.
- [Relative speed (chase and meeting)](/formulas/time-speed-distance#relative-speed): $\text{same direction: } t=\frac{\text{gap}}{v_1-v_2};\ \text{opposite: } t=\frac d{v_1+v_2}$
  - Shortcut: Distance the leader runs before being caught $=\text{gap}\times\frac{v_2}{v_1-v_2}$ (speed-ratio units).
- [Speed and time inversely proportional](/formulas/time-speed-distance#speed-time-inverse): $\text{fixed } d:\ \frac{t_1}{t_2}=\frac{v_2}{v_1};\ \text{fixed } t:\ \frac{d_1}{d_2}=\frac{v_1}{v_2}$
  - Shortcut: Swap the ratio: times $a:b$ over the same distance mean speeds $b:a$.
- [Meeting on a circular track](/formulas/time-speed-distance#circular-track): $t_{\text{first meet}}=\frac{L}{|v_1\mp v_2|}\ (- \text{ same direction},\ + \text{ opposite});\ \text{at start: } \mathrm{LCM}\left(\tfrac L{v_1},\tfrac L{v_2}\right)$
  - Shortcut: Same direction: time $=\frac{L}{\text{difference}}$; opposite: $\frac{L}{\text{sum}}$; convert km/h with $\times\frac5{18}$ first.
- [Boats and streams](/formulas/time-speed-distance#boats-streams): $D=b+s,\ U=b-s;\ b=\frac{D+U}2,\ s=\frac{D-U}2$
  - Shortcut: Still-water speed is the average of $D$ and $U$; stream speed is half their difference.
- [Trains crossing](/formulas/time-speed-distance#train-crossing): $t=\frac{L_1+L_2}{v_1\pm v_2}\ (+\text{ opposite},\ -\text{ same direction})$
  - Shortcut: Same-direction and opposite times $t_s,t_o$ give $v_1-v_2=\frac{L_1+L_2}{t_s}$ and $v_1+v_2=\frac{L_1+L_2}{t_o}$.
- [Average speed over equal distances](/formulas/time-speed-distance#average-speed-equal-distance): $\bar v=\frac{2v_1v_2}{v_1+v_2};\ n\text{ equal distances: }\bar v=\frac{n}{\Sigma\frac1{v_i}}$
  - Shortcut: Take the distance as the LCM of the speeds, add the times, divide.
