# Data Interpretation: cheat sheet

- [Read values and totals](/formulas/data-interpretation#read-and-total): $\text{Read the needed cells/bars, add row or column totals, compare or rank}$
  - Shortcut: Difference of totals = total of differences: add the Total column and the Present column for a year, then subtract once.
- [Averages from a chart](/formulas/data-interpretation#average-from-chart): $\text{average}=\frac{\text{sum of selected values}}{\text{count}};\ \text{compare averages via totals when counts are equal}$
  - Shortcut: Equal counts: compare totals (or sums of deviations from a round base) instead of averages.
- [Percentages and percent change from a chart](/formulas/data-interpretation#percentage-from-chart): $\text{share}\%=\frac{\text{value}}{\text{total}}\times100;\ \%\Delta=\frac{V_2-V_1}{V_1}\times100$
  - Shortcut: Convert the percent to a target value once ($79\%$ of $500=395$), then scan the totals instead of dividing each one.
- [Ratios from a chart](/formulas/data-interpretation#ratio-from-chart): $\text{ratio}=\frac{\Sigma\text{ selected } A}{\Sigma\text{ selected } B} \text{ simplified}$
  - Shortcut: Divide every bar by the common step first (here 25): $(14+15+8):(6+12+10)=37:28$.
- [Derived quantities from given relations](/formulas/data-interpretation#derived-quantity): $\text{Combine table values with a stated relation (e.g. income}=\text{expenditure}\times(1+p)) \text{ to find the unknown}$
  - Shortcut: Income $=E\times(1+p/100)$: one multiplier, e.g. $55\%$ profit means $\times1.55$.
- [Pie chart central angles](/formulas/data-interpretation#pie-central-angle): $\theta=\frac{\%}{100}\times360^\circ;\ \text{value}=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times\text{total}$
  - Shortcut: $1\%=3.6^\circ$; $25\%=90^\circ$, $50\%=180^\circ$. Ignore the total when only angles or comparisons are asked.
- [Pie chart percent to value](/formulas/data-interpretation#pie-percent-value): $\text{value}=\frac{\%}{100}\times\text{total};\ \text{difference of sectors}=\frac{\%_1-\%_2}{100}\times\text{total}$
  - Shortcut: Multiply the percents: $30\%\times70\%$ of $20000=21\%$ of $20000=4200$.
