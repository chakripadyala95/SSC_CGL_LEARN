# Mixture & Alligation: cheat sheet

- [Rule of alligation](/formulas/mixture-alligation#alligation-rule): $\frac{q_{\text{cheap}}}{q_{\text{dear}}}=\frac{d-m}{m-c}$
- [Mean price of a mixture](/formulas/mixture-alligation#weighted-mean-price): $m=\frac{q_1c_1+q_2c_2}{q_1+q_2}$
  - Shortcut: $m = c_1 + (c_2 - c_1)\frac{q_2}{q_1 + q_2}$: start at the first price and move by the second part's share.
- [Adding to or combining mixtures](/formulas/mixture-alligation#add-to-mixture): $\frac{a+x}{b}=\frac pq;\ \text{combined component}_k=\Sigma_i V_i\frac{r_{ik}}{\Sigma_j r_{ij}}$
  - Shortcut: Hold the untouched component fixed and scale the new ratio to it; the gap in the other component is the amount to add.
- [Repeated replacement](/formulas/mixture-alligation#repeated-replacement): $\text{left}=V\left(1-\frac xV\right)^n$
  - Shortcut: Fraction left after $n$ steps $= \left(1 - \frac xV\right)^n$; e.g. drawing $\frac1{10}$ three times leaves $0.9^3 = 72.9\%$.
