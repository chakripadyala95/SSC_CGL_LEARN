# Simplification: cheat sheet

- [VBODMAS order of operations](/formulas/simplification#bodmas): $\text{Vinculum} \to (\,)\{\,\}[\,] \to \text{Of} \to \div,\times\ (\text{left to right}) \to +,-\ (\text{left to right});\ a\div b \text{ of } c=\frac{a}{bc}$
  - Shortcut: Turn each $\div x$ into $\times\frac1x$ and each $-x$ into $+(-x)$; then order no longer matters within a rank.
- [Fraction and decimal arithmetic](/formulas/simplification#fraction-decimal-operations): $\frac ab \pm \frac cd=\frac{ad\pm bc}{bd};\ \text{convert mixed numbers and decimals to fractions before combining}$
  - Shortcut: Use $\mathrm{LCM}(b,d)$ as the denominator to keep numbers small; for decimals, shift both by the same power of 10 before dividing ($\frac{1.64}{0.04} = \frac{164}{4}$).
- [Rationalising surds](/formulas/simplification#surd-rationalisation): $\frac{1}{\sqrt a+\sqrt b}=\frac{\sqrt a-\sqrt b}{a-b}$
  - Shortcut: When $a - b = 1$: $\frac{1}{\sqrt a + \sqrt b} = \sqrt a - \sqrt b$; and $\frac xy + \frac yx = \frac{x^2+y^2}{xy}$ avoids rationalising twice.
