# Direction: cheat sheet

- [Net displacement](/formulas/direction#net-displacement): $d=\sqrt{(\text{net N-S})^2+(\text{net E-W})^2}$
  - Shortcut: Look for 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17 triples in the two nets.
- [Turn tracking](/formulas/direction#turn-tracking): $\text{Right turn = 90° clockwise, left turn = 90° anticlockwise; update the facing direction after every turn.}$
  - Shortcut: Count net turns: (rights $-$ lefts) mod 4 steps clockwise from the start direction.
