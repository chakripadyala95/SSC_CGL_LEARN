# SI/CI: cheat sheet

- [Simple interest](/formulas/si-ci#simple-interest): $SI=\frac{PRT}{100};\ A=P\left(1+\frac{RT}{100}\right);\ \text{equal interest on split: } P_1R_1T_1=P_2R_2T_2$
  - Shortcut: Interest per year $=R\%$ of $P$; multiply by years. Months: $T=\frac{m}{12}$. Split for equal interest: $P_1:P_2=\frac1{R_1T_1}:\frac1{R_2T_2}$.
- [Instalments and reducing principal](/formulas/si-ci#instalment-reducing-principal): $\text{interest each period on outstanding principal; equal instalment } x:\ \Sigma x\left(1+\tfrac{R t_k}{100}\right)=A,\ t_k=\text{years left after instalment }k$
  - Shortcut: Final payment $=(P-x)\left(1+\frac R{100}\right)+\frac{PR}{100}$.
- [Present worth / true discount](/formulas/si-ci#present-value): $PW=\frac{100A}{100+RT};\ TD=A-PW$
  - Shortcut: $TD=\frac{A\cdot RT}{100+RT}$; $\ TD$ is the SI on $PW$.
- [Compound interest amount](/formulas/si-ci#compound-interest): $A=P\left(1+\frac{R/k}{100}\right)^{nk}\ (k=1,2,4 \text{ per year}),\ CI=A-P$
  - Shortcut: Successive change: $R\%$ for 2 years $=2R+\frac{R^2}{100}\%$ ($10\%\to21\%$).
- [CI - SI for 2 and 3 years](/formulas/si-ci#ci-si-difference): $2\text{ yr}: P\left(\frac R{100}\right)^2;\ 3\text{ yr}: P\left(\frac R{100}\right)^2\left(3+\frac R{100}\right)$
  - Shortcut: 2 years: difference $=P\times\left(\frac R{100}\right)^2$, e.g. ₹10,000 at $10\%$ gives ₹100.
