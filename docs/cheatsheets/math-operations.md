# Mathematical Operations: cheat sheet

- [Sign/symbol interchange](/formulas/math-operations#sign-interchange): $\text{Rewrite first: swap each pair of signs everywhere, then evaluate by BODMAS.}$
  - Shortcut: Do the $\times$ and $\div$ parts first and the unit digits of the result often decide the option.
- [BODMAS evaluation](/formulas/math-operations#bodmas): $\text{Brackets}\to\text{Of}\to(\div,\times\ \text{left to right})\to(+,-\ \text{left to right})$
- [Swap trial to balance equation](/formulas/math-operations#swap-to-balance): $\text{For each option: apply the swap, evaluate LHS by BODMAS, keep the option with LHS = RHS.}$
