# Profit & Loss: cheat sheet

- [Profit / loss percent and CP from SP](/formulas/profit-loss#profit-percent): $P\%=\frac{SP-CP}{CP}\times100;\ SP=CP\left(1\pm\frac P{100}\right) \iff CP=\frac{SP}{1\pm P/100}$
  - Shortcut: Take $CP = 100$: $SP = 100 + P$, so $CP : SP = 100 : (100+P)$.
- [Gain as a fraction of SP](/formulas/profit-loss#gain-fraction-of-sp): $\text{If gain}=\tfrac{a}{b}SP \text{ then } CP=\tfrac{b-a}{b}SP,\ \text{gain}\%=\frac{a}{b-a}\times100$
  - Shortcut: Gain $g\%$ of SP $\Rightarrow$ gain $\frac{g}{100-g}\times100\%$ of CP (1/6 of SP ⇒ 1/5 = 20% of CP).
- [Overall gain on parts sold at different rates](/formulas/profit-loss#overall-gain): $\text{overall }\%=\frac{\Sigma CP_i\,p_i}{\Sigma CP_i};\ \text{target SP of rest}=\text{target total}-\text{SP already realised}$
  - Shortcut: Equal cost per article: overall % = (fraction sold at $p_1$)$\times p_1$ + (rest)$\times p_2$.
- [Discount on marked price](/formulas/profit-loss#discount-on-marked-price): $SP=MP\left(1-\frac d{100}\right);\ d\%=\frac{MP-SP}{MP}\times100$
  - Shortcut: $MP = SP\times\frac{100}{100-d}$ (₹1,792 after 20% off: $1792\times\frac{100}{80}$).
- [Markup then discount](/formulas/profit-loss#markup-discount): $\frac{SP}{CP}=\left(1+\frac m{100}\right)\left(1-\frac d{100}\right);\ \frac{MP}{CP}=\frac{100+p}{100-d}$
  - Shortcut: Net profit % $= m - d - \frac{md}{100}$ (successive change with $+m$, $-d$).
- [Successive discounts / gains](/formulas/profit-loss#successive-discount): $\text{single equivalent of } d_1,d_2=d_1+d_2-\frac{d_1d_2}{100};\ \text{successive gains } a,b: a+b+\frac{ab}{100}$
  - Shortcut: Remaining-fraction method: multiply $(100-d_1)\%$ by $(100-d_2)\%$, e.g. $0.85\times0.8 = 0.68$ ⇒ 32% off.
- [False weights](/formulas/profit-loss#false-weight): $\text{gain}\%=\frac{\text{true}-\text{false}}{\text{false}}\times100;\ \text{with profit } p:\ \frac{SP}{CP}=(1+p)\frac{\text{true}}{\text{false}}$
  - Shortcut: Gain % = $\frac{\text{error}}{\text{true value} - \text{error}}\times100$ (200 g short per kg ⇒ $\frac{200}{800} = 25\%$).
