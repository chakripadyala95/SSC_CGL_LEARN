# Statistics: cheat sheet

- [Mean of grouped data](/formulas/statistics#mean): $\bar x=\frac{\Sigma f_ix_i}{\Sigma f_i}$
  - Shortcut: Assumed mean: $\bar x = A + \frac{\Sigma f_i(x_i - A)}{\Sigma f_i}$; symmetric frequencies give $\bar x$ = the middle value.
- [Mode and the empirical relation](/formulas/statistics#mode): $\text{mode}=\text{most frequent value};\ \text{mode}=3\,\text{median}-2\,\text{mean}\ (\text{symmetric: all equal})$
  - Shortcut: Use 3 median $-$ 2 mean only when the question says 'empirical relation' or gives two of the three.
- [Median](/formulas/statistics#median): $\text{sorted data: middle value } \left(\tfrac{n+1}2\right)^{\text{th}};\ \text{even } n: \text{ mean of the two middle values}$
- [Range and standard deviation](/formulas/statistics#range-deviation): $\text{range}=\max-\min;\ \sigma=\sqrt{\frac{\Sigma(x_i-\bar x)^2}{n}}$
  - Shortcut: $\sigma^2 = \overline{x^2} - \bar x^2$; adding a constant changes neither range nor $\sigma$, multiplying by $k$ multiplies both by $|k|$.
